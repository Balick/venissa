## Composition

The purple sky and distant hills carry the opening message. Small points of light guide the eye down to the next panel without a hard change of scene.

## Design detail

Dark image backgrounds need room for type. The composition leaves open sky around the headline and keeps supporting text away from the illustrated detail.
