## Composition

Illustrated categories offer a quick way into the menu. Dish cards keep the image, name and price close together, so browsing requires little backtracking.

## Design detail

The order panel sits beside the menu. Its separate background makes the boundary between choosing a dish and reviewing the basket visible.
