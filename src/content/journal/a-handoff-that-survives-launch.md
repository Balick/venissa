category: Web development

---

## Make one real edit together

Ask the next editor to add a project, including its title, image description and summary. Follow the published instructions exactly. Any step that needs an explanation belongs in the documentation or in the template.

## Review the result at two widths

Open the new detail page and the listing on a phone and a wide screen. A long title or unusually tall image can reveal a layout assumption that the first few examples never tested.
