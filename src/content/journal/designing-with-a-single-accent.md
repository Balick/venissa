## Assign the color a job

On a neutral page, color attracts attention quickly. Start with the primary action, then repeat the accent in a few smaller details that connect the sections. A neutral secondary button keeps the primary choice easy to find.

## Check the quiet states

Inspect the page in grayscale and navigate with a keyboard. Links still need recognizable labels, focused controls need a visible outline, and selected filters need a cue beyond a change of hue.
